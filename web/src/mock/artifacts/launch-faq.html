Almanac 2.0

Launch FAQ

Answers for the team ahead of the 1 December launch.

Pricing

What does the unlimited plan cost?

$34 a month, or $29 a month billed yearly. The $39 price tested in Q3 lost too much conversion on mobile and is not planned.

Do current customers change price?

No. Existing subscriptions keep their price until the plan is renewed after 1 March.

Product

What is new in Almanac 2.0?

Search across every connected archive, shared pages that agents publish, and a faster model runner. Release notes are in the November changelog.

Which agents can publish pages?

Any agent in the workspace with the artifacts feature on. Pages are private to the workspace until the owner shares them.

Where do I report a problem?

Post in #launch with the page address. Include the version number from the page's Versions tab.

Customers

Who can see the Larkspur result?

Only people the owner added. The public summary leaves out channel spend.
