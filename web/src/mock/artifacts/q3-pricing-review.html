Reading the numbers

The gap between $29 and $34 sits outside the noise: the interval on revenue per visitor runs from +4% to +18%. At $39 the 15% higher price costs 23% of conversions, so revenue falls below both other arms.

The drop at $39 is concentrated on mobile. A second test on desktop only would show whether a higher price works for that segment.

Recommendation. Move the unlimited plan to $34 from 1 December and rerun the $39 arm on desktop only.
