- **A routine that posts in a thread under its channel adds no closing note there.** A run
  that opened a thread in its destination channel, or posted into one, used to get its final
  words ("I posted the check-in thread…") posted to the channel as well. A thread under the
  channel now counts as the routine posting for itself, as a post to the channel already did.
  Daimon keeps the final reply in the run's record and posts nothing more, and the run is told
  to post everything meant for the channel itself
  ([Routines](docs/routines.md#delivery)).
